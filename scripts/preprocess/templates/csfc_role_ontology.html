Keep associations separate

Links such as “administers” and “may be realized as” preserve documented relationships. They are annotations in OWL and do not impose a relationship on every member of a class.

What the ontology leaves unasserted

The organizing root and topic filters help navigation. Missing relationships remain unknown. The export asserts no class equivalence, disjointness, deployed device instances, or CSfC compliance.
